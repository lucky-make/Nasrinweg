Welcome to Köln

You've just landed. Over 14 days, you'll practice German through retrieval — no lists, no rereading.

The 5 Laws:

  1. Guess first. The German is hidden until you try.
  2. Things come back when you are about to forget them.
  3. Question types are mixed. Notice what each one wants.
  4. You will explain each new rule in your own words, board closed.
  5. You learn useful wholes: Ich hätte gern … — not three orphan words.
